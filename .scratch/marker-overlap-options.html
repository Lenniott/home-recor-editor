1. Your hatch directions + typed rails

Silence stays the diagonal you already know. Cut is vertical stripes. Export is horizontal (vertical was already taken by cut). Fill still tints by type. Rails are opaque type color.

2. Opposite diagonals, vertical export

Silence / , cut \ , export |. Three orientations, none shared. Same opaque rails. Overlap reads as a woven plaid instead of a mud puddle.

3. One shared fill, hatches and rails do the talking

One grey band wherever any mark exists, so stacked fills don't go dark. Type only shows in hatch direction and in the solid start/end colors sitting on top.
